    Your answers

    How the estimate works. About 44,000 dictionary words (CC-CEDICT entries, excluding names) are ranked by how often they appear in written and spoken Chinese (wordfreq corpus) and cut into frequency bands. A few opening questions spread across the bands; after that, each question is aimed where you're about as likely to know a word as not, which is where an answer tells the most. All your answers are fitted to a curve of how your knowledge falls off with rarity. The estimate is the number of words under that curve, and the likely range covers 90% of the curves that fit your answers.

    Limits. Frequency rank is a rough proxy for difficulty, single characters are counted but not quizzed, and a retake will usually land within the range shown. Words beyond rank 40,000 are not counted, so very advanced learners will be underestimated. HSK comparisons use word-list size only.
